### deferred — An off switch for dual-use knowledge in AI models (GRAM)
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/off-switch-dual-use
- Source: anthropic.com/research, published 2026-07-08
- Theme: 3 — Constitutional / safety primitives
- Touches: `csis/safety/tier_guard.py`, `CSIS-architecture.html` (L7 safety envelope section)
- Size: medium
- Risk: medium
- Design sketch: GRAM gives a model dedicated, removable per-category compartments (virology, cybersecurity, nuclear physics, etc.) so dangerous knowledge can be physically excised at deployment while leaving general capability intact. GRAM itself is a *training-time* technique CSIS's codebase can't adopt directly — CSIS calls existing model checkpoints over the API, it doesn't train weights. But the underlying principle (compartmentalize by category, gate at the boundary) is already the shape of `TierGuard`, which today gates by **role** (`coordinator`/`researcher`/`builder`/... → a single `CapabilityTier` ceiling) rather than by **domain**. The sketch: extend `TierGuard` with a second axis — a per-domain-adapter ceiling (`pr_maintenance` / `self_improve` / `lean_math` today, more later) — so a role's *effective* ceiling is `min(role_ceiling, domain_ceiling)`, mirroring GRAM's per-category compartments at the orchestration layer instead of the weight layer. `_CONSUMER_TIER` and `_role_ceiling` in `csis/safety/tier_guard.py:16-45` are the chokepoint; `TierGuard.authorize()` and `.write_tier()` are the two call sites that would need the second lookup.
- Why deferred: `tier_guard.py` is safety-adjacent even though it isn't one of the four named cycle-9 chokepoints (`Coordinator.__init__`, `_BackendTracker`, `writer_iteration_id`, promotion CAS) — cycle-9 discipline says don't touch code like this without a regression test that fails before and passes after, and a two-axis ceiling changes `authorize()`'s contract for every existing caller. Also genuinely unclear whether "domain ceiling" should live in `TierGuard` itself or in each domain adapter's `can_run()` — that's a design call worth a maintainer's input before code, not something to decide solo in a routine run.
