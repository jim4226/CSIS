### deferred — Claude's values across models and languages
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-values-models-languages
- Source: anthropic.com/research, published 2026-07-13
- Theme: 3 (Constitutional / safety primitives)
- Touches: `csis/safety/tripwires.py` (new probe type) or a new `csis/safety/value_drift.py`; dashboard wiring in `csis/ui/`
- Size: medium
- Risk: low-medium
- Design sketch: Anthropic's methodology extracts four interpretable value axes (deference/caution, warmth/rigor, depth/brevity, candor/execution) from a large sample of Claude.ai conversations, then tracks how they shift across models and languages. CSIS could adapt the shape of this — not the exact axes — as a periodic probe: after N promoted iterations, sample recent V2 critic rationale text and Builder why-docs, score them along a small set of CSIS-relevant axes (e.g., permissiveness vs. caution in cert decisions), and diff against a Phase-0 baseline snapshot. A drift past threshold fires a non-blocking tripwire event surfaced on the dashboard's existing "Tripwire firings" panel, rather than gating promotion outright.
- Why deferred: this is new safety-monitoring surface area, not a fix to something broken — it needs its own design pass on what "axes" mean for CSIS's structured artifacts/certs (which aren't free-text conversations like the source study), plus a regression test proving it doesn't false-positive against the existing 246-test corpus before it's safe to land. That's more than a same-day trivial/small PR, and the risk tier depends on exactly where the probe hooks into the Auditor path, which needs its own review.
