### opened-PR — Paving the way for agents in biology
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-in-biology
- Source: anthropic.com/research, published 2026-06-08
- Theme: 2 (Trust + verification)
- Touches: `csis/contracts.py` (GraderResult.provenance), `csis/verification/graders.py`, `tests/test_verification.py`
- Size: small
- Risk: low
- PR: #42

The paper introduced `gget virus`, a deterministic execution layer wrapping the unreliable NCBI Virus portal. Its key property: it returns **provenance logs** alongside every result (what commands ran, what inputs, in what order), enabling downstream agents to verify *how* data was retrieved. Without it, four state-of-the-art agents ranged 17–91% accuracy with high run-to-run variance; with it, all reached >90% and model choice became much less important.

In CSIS terms: V1 graders already pin source-hashes (F6) to detect code drift, but they don’t record execution traces. Adding `GraderResult.provenance` closes that gap. The chokepoint is `GraderRegistry.evaluate()` — the single call site where all pinned graders run and their results are collected. Threading provenance through there propagates to the Verifier and VerifierCertificate without touching Coordinator, `_BackendTracker`, `writer_iteration_id`, or the promotion CAS.
