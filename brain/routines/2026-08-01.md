### deferred — Discovering cryptographic weaknesses with Claude
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses
- Source: anthropic.com/research, published 2026-07-28
- Theme: 7 (Curiosity / frontier-item generation) — also touches 5 (self-improvement loops)
- Touches: `csis/curiosity.py` (frontier-item generator)
- Size: medium+
- Risk: low-medium (doesn't touch a cycle-9 chokepoint directly, but changes the shape of what a frontier item carries, which propagates to Researcher/Builder/Verifier)
- Design sketch: the research describes Claude Mythos Preview autonomously improving attacks on HAWK and reduced-round AES over many sessions via hypothesis-driven experimentation and self-authored "autonomous validation chains" that it used to convince itself (and, later, human researchers) a result was correct before handing it off. CSIS's `curiosity.py` currently emits a single frontier item per cycle with no multi-round refinement or self-authored validation artifact attached. A Phase-1-shaped change would let a frontier item carry an optional `validation_chain: list[str]` — a trail of the Researcher's own intermediate hypotheses and how each was checked — that the Verifier can inspect as *evidence*, not just as a rubric-pass/fail input. This is squarely in "the eval output shapes the next step" territory the README already claims as CSIS's thesis; the novelty here is applying it to curiosity generation, not just verification.
- Why deferred: this changes the `FrontierItem` contract shape (a new field flowing through Researcher → Builder → Verifier), which is more than a same-day trivial addition and deserves review against the existing V1/V2 cert flow rather than a same-day PR.
