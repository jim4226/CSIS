  `csis/verification/certificates.py` — the only site where a `VerifierCertificate` is assembled.
  The paper's empirical finding (28–33% verified success for intermediate-expert domains, 15% for

  promotion CAS. The `verifier_confidence` field is `Optional[float]`; every existing call site
  passes `calibration=None` (default) and receives `verifier_confidence=None`, unchanged.
  Gating promotion on the threshold is intentionally left to P1.5 completion.
