### deferred — Cyber Jailbreak Severity (CJS) framework → tripwire severity scoring
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-safeguards-jailbreak-framework
- Source: anthropic.com/news, published 2026-07-02
- Theme: 3 — Constitutional / safety primitives
- Touches: `csis/safety/tripwires.py` (`TripwireFiring`, `TripwireResult`, `Tripwires.scan_text`)
- Size: medium
- Risk: medium
- Design sketch: The article describes a four-axis severity rubric for Fable 5's cyber-jailbreak classifier — capability gain (0–4), breadth (0–2), ease of weaponization (0–2), discoverability (0–2), summed to a 0–10 score mapped to Informational/Low/Medium/High/Critical levels, with scores only ever raised (never lowered) on discretionary review. CSIS's tripwires today are pure binary fired/not-fired (`csis/safety/tripwires.py:127-137`). A CSIS-shaped analog: add an optional `severity: int` field to `TripwireFiring`, score each `_TRIP_PATTERNS` entry along similar axes, and let the Coordinator's halt policy vary by level (e.g. only Medium+ pages the Overseer; Low/Informational just log). The rubric itself (four independent 0–2/0–4 axes summed) is simple to port; the risk is entirely in what changes downstream of a firing.
- Why deferred: this changes when the Coordinator actually halts and pages the Overseer — real safety-critical behavior, not just observability — which is exactly the class of change CYCLES.md says needs its own red-team-style critique pass (see cycles 6–9), not a same-day PR alongside three other unrelated changes.

Worth revisiting when P1.1 is actually picked up rather than piecemeal today.
