Anthropic's Frontier Red Team analyzed 832 banned accounts (March 2025–March
2026). Key finding: "agentic orchestration" — AI autonomously chaining attack
stages without human intervention — is the highest-risk pattern and is NOT
captured by MITRE ATT&CK. Medium-to-high risk actors using this pattern grew
from 33% to 56% over the period. Traditional risk signals (number of
techniques, platform) no longer reliably identify threat level.

Three sub-patterns added to `_TRIP_PATTERNS["agentic_attack_orchestration"]`:
- A: `autonomous/autonomously` + offensive action within 60 chars
- B: `chain/orchestrate/automate` + offensive stages (the MITRE-named gap)
- C: `without human oversight/intervention/supervision` + offensive action

10 fire cases + 5 clean cases parametrized; all 265 suite tests pass.
