- **Recommendation for the human operator**: either (a) periodically merge or close the backlog of `claude/daily-*` draft PRs so `index.md`/day-logs actually land on `main` and the dedup mechanism starts working as designed, or (b) if the intent is for these to stay as a permanent unmerged proposal queue, change Step 1 of the routine to also cross-check the live open-PR list (title/slug) rather than relying solely on `brain/routines/index.md`, since that file is currently inert.
