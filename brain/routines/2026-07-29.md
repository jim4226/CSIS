### deferred — HAWK/AES cryptographic red-team methodology (hypothesis → experiment → verify loop)
- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses
- Source: anthropic.com/research (Frontier Red Team), published 2026-07-28
- Theme: 7 (Curiosity / frontier-item generation)
- Touches: `csis/curiosity.py` (`FrontierItem` generator)
- Size: medium
- Risk: low
- Design sketch: the AES-7 discovery scaffold had Claude explicitly "pose hypotheses, run experiments to validate or refute them, then design an attack" — and needed nearly a month of independent human verification after a week of discovery, i.e. discovery and verification were treated as separate, asymmetric-cost stages. A `hypothesis` + `expected_evidence` field on `FrontierItem`, checked by V2's falsifying critic before the Builder invests effort, would mirror this pattern and bias curiosity generation toward falsifiable claims instead of open-ended "try something" items.
- Why deferred: touches the curiosity → verification interface, which several downstream consumers (Researcher prompt templates, V2 critic rubric) depend on — needs a proper design pass, not a same-day PR.
